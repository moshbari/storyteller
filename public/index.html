Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What is an "AI agent" and why is this like OpenClaw?▼

OpenClaw showed the world what autonomous AI agents can do — AI that actually does things, not just talks. StoryTeller uses the same concept: three specialized AI agents that autonomously write, illustrate, and assemble complete children's books. You click, they deliver. No prompting, no manual work.

Do I need to write anything?▼

No. You can pick from 100+ done-for-you story ideas with one click. Or hit "Surprise Me" and the agent selects everything for you — topic, art style, mood, everything. Zero writing required.

How long does it take to create a book?▼

About 60-90 seconds for a 5-page book and under 2 minutes for a 12-page book. You can watch all three agents working in real-time during creation.

Can I sell the books I create?▼

Yes! Full commercial rights on every book. Many users sell on Amazon KDP, Etsy, and other platforms. We export in Amazon's exact KDP format — one click to download, one click to upload.

Do my credits expire?▼

Never. One-time purchase, use whenever you want. No subscriptions, no monthly limits.

Is there a free trial?▼

Yes! 3 free books, no credit card needed. Your agents are ready right now — create your first book in three clicks.
